FP32 vs Posit32 GCN precision audit
===================================

FP32 trainer:
- Inputs, edge weights, parameters, forward pass and logits: FP32
- Loss/softmax: double intermediates
- Backpropagation gradients: FP32
- Weight updates: FP32 arithmetic
- Saved parameters: float32 serialization

Posit32 trainer:
- Inputs, edge weights, parameters, forward pass and logits: Posit32<32,2>
- Loss/softmax: double intermediates
- Backpropagation gradients: mostly double
- Weight updates: gradients converted to Posit32 before update arithmetic
- Saved parameters: float32 serialization

Current classification:
Mixed-precision training implementations; not fully native-format
end-to-end training.

Important:
Compare identical initialization, data splits, training policy and
evaluation procedure before drawing precision conclusions.
